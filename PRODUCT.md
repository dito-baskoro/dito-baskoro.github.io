# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors arriving at Dito Baskoro's personal site: recruiters, hiring managers, founders, agency leads, and fellow developers who land here from a CV link, a social profile, or a referral. They show up curious, time-pressed, and skimming. Their unspoken question is "who is this person, are they any good, and how do I reach them." Context is a quick desktop or mobile glance, often between other tabs.

## Product Purpose

A personal calling card: a polished, single-page introduction to Dito, a creative developer based in Jakarta. Success is a visitor leaving with a clear sense of identity and craft, and an easy path to make contact. It is not a lead-funnel or a conversion machine; it is a memorable, well-built artifact that earns trust by being itself.

## Brand Personality

Creative and expressive, built on a technical foundation. Three words: confident, crafted, alive. The voice is first-person and direct, not corporate. The site should feel like it was hand-built by someone with taste, not assembled from a template. Personality and motion are welcome where they serve the impression; restraint is welcome where it sharpens it.

## Anti-references

- **Generic dev portfolio.** The interchangeable dark-theme + neon-accent + tech-grid template every developer ships. If a visitor could swap the name and not notice, it has failed.
- **Corporate SaaS landing.** Hero-metric blocks, identical feature-card grids, stock gradients, marketing-speak. This is a person, not a product page.
- **Template marketplace look.** Themeforest/Wix-shaped layouts that read as bought rather than built.

## Design Principles

1. **Built, not bought.** Every choice should signal a person who writes code with care. The medium is part of the message.
2. **Expressive on a technical spine.** Keep the developer-native bones (mono type, code motifs, precise grid) but push past the safe version with bolder typography, motion, and composition. Evolve the current direction; don't settle for the first-reflex dev-portfolio look.
3. **Earn attention, then reward it.** A strong first impression that holds up on a second, closer read. Details survive scrutiny.
4. **One clear way to reach me.** Identity and contact are never more than a glance away. Low pressure, high clarity.
5. **Craft survives scrutiny.** Animations, spacing, and states should feel intentional under inspection, never decorative filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid defaults: WCAG AA contrast (the lime accent on near-black currently passes), keyboard-navigable links with visible focus, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for the GSAP loading and entrance sequences. Motion is an enhancement, never a gate.
